# Quant backtesting

Specify input availability, decision time, order execution and return accrual. A signal produced after an interval cannot earn that interval's return.

Keep signals, executed positions, turnover, gross PnL, costs and net PnL distinct. Configure transaction costs and slippage; state their units. Use fills supported by the data and market mechanics. Reject impossible prices or volume. Define applicable cash, leverage, financing, corporate-action and rebalancing rules. Test timing and accounting with hand-computable synthetic cases.
