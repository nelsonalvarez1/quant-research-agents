# Alpha research

Explore momentum, mean reversion, volatility, cross-sectional signals, spreads, relative value and regime filters as unproven candidates. Specify each candidate's mechanism, data, timing, universe, cost and capacity risks, and falsifying test. Start with one simple baseline and predefine a limited comparison set. Separate exploratory results from final holdout evidence. In-sample profitability does not establish alpha.
